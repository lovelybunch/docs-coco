Templates make solutions repeatable. A template is an ordinary page under a space's `templates/` prefix, versioned, ACL'd, and editable like any other page. What makes it a template is its frontmatter.

```yaml
description: Investment memo starting point        # shown in the picker
metadataDefaults:                                  # applied as metadata on create
  stage: sourcing
  conviction-score: 0.5
```

A template that also declares a `metadataSchema` becomes a [record type](/concepts/records): its pages are validated on create and queryable as rows. That page covers schemas, field types, and validation in full.

## Page templates

- **Create one** at `templates/<name>` in the space, using the shortcut on the space manage page. Write the starting content, then add the frontmatter above.
- **Use one** from the New Page form, which shows a "Start from" picker listing every template you can read, org-wide. Choosing one pre-fills the content and title, and `metadataDefaults` are applied after creation so the new page is immediately queryable in [Views](/concepts/views).
- **List them** with `GET /templates`, optionally scoped with `?space=<slug>&includeContent=true`.

Combined with [export and import](/concepts/export-import), a space of templates _is_ a distributable solution kit. Export the space, import it into another deployment, and the templates come with it.

## Space templates

Page templates seed one page. Space templates seed the whole space.

The `/templates` gallery in the app lists ready-made kits, with their pages, page templates, and agent instructions included. "Use this template" creates a new space with everything in place. A space template's content is exactly the export bundle format, so authoring one means building a space, exporting it, and adding it to a catalog.

Templates come from three sources, all merged into one gallery:

## API

| Method and path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /space-templates` | The merged gallery |
| `POST /space-templates/:source/:id/spaces` | Create a space from a template |
| `POST /space-templates/org` | Snapshot a space as an org template |
| `DELETE /space-templates/org/:id` | Remove an org template |

Marketing entry points can deep-link `/templates?template=<id>`, and the destination survives sign-in and org onboarding.
