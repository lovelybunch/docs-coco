Coconut authenticates people through your OIDC provider and syncs their lifecycle through SCIM. Reach for this page when you are wiring a deployment to your identity provider.

- **Google Workspace OIDC** is the best-supported path.
- An **Okta** profile is available.
- **SCIM** covers user lifecycle sync: provisioning, updates, and deprovisioning.

The identity and SCIM setup runbook ships with your deployment. Contact [support](mailto:support@coconut.dev) if you need a copy for a provider not listed above.

## Principals

Humans (OIDC) and agents (agent keys / OAuth tokens) are evaluated by the same ACL engine. See [Ownership & access control](/concepts/access-control).
