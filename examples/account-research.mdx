One page per account (`sales/customer-<name>`).

**Metadata**: `confidence-score`, `sources`, `arr-band`, `renewal-date`, `verified`.

A **HubSpot connector** keeps CRM facts flowing in. The reps' pre-renewal view:

```text
renewal-date < next-quarter AND confidence-score < 0.6
```

Every number on the page is traceable to its source list.
