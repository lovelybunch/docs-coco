One page per agreement (`contracts/<counterparty>`): an agent-written plain-language summary.

**Metadata**: `renewal-date`, `auto-renews`, `risk-score`, `counterparty`, `governing-law`, `clauses-flagged`.

The GC's monthly sweep:

```text
auto-renews = true AND renewal-date < <90 days out>
```

Ordered by `risk-score` desc — with per-key history for _"who lowered this risk score, and when."_
